Mirror Dashboard Privacy Policy

Mirror Dashboard is a private, self-hosted smart mirror application intended for personal use.

Google Calendar Data

With the owner's authorization, Mirror Dashboard accesses Google Calendar information to display selected calendars and upcoming events.

The application requests read-only Calendar permissions and does not create, modify, or delete Google Calendar events.

Data Storage and Sharing

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's device. Calendar information is processed for display on the private dashboard and is not intentionally published or sold to third parties.

Revoking Access

The owner can revoke Google Calendar access at any time through Google Account security settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the support email displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
